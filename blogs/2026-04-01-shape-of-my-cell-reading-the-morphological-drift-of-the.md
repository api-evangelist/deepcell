The UK’s National Measurement Laboratory (NML) at LGC is leading a shift in how we define "healthy" cells. In collaboration with Deepcell, Dr. Jeanne Rivera and her team are applying digital metrology to identify "morphological drift"—subtle, AI-detected changes in cell shape and structure that human eyes and standard assays miss.
