For decades, Cell Line Development (CLD) has been a waiting game. New research from Gilead Sciences and the Science for Life Laboratory suggests that the wait is over. By leveraging generative AI and "digital cartography," scientists are now identifying high-producing clones in 36 days instead of 43—predicting a cell's destiny from the very first frame of imaging.
